WHY GHOST FORM

Built Different. Built Private.

🧠

On-Device ML

A quantized transformer model runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly. No cloud inference. No data ever leaves your machine.

🎭

Zero-Day Detection

Blacklists fail against brand-new phishing domains. Ghost Form uses semantic similarity — it detects fake PayPal pages even if they're 10 seconds old.

🔍

Shadow DOM Aware

Modern phishing kits hide inputs inside Shadow DOMs and iframes. Ghost Form penetrates both, attaching listeners where other extensions go blind.

THE PROCESS

Three Steps. Instant Protection.

Everything happens in milliseconds, entirely on your device.

🔬
STEP 01

Semantic Analysis

The ML model embeds the page's text and URL into a 384-dimensional vector. It computes cosine similarity against known brand signatures like PayPal, Google, and Amazon — all locally.

🚦
STEP 02

Trust Indicators

Form fields get color-coded trust states — Green (Verified), Yellow (Unknown), or Red (Unsafe) — before you type anything sensitive.

🔒
STEP 03

Data Stays Local

The regex engine that detects credit card formats and password patterns runs entirely in memory. Zero keystrokes are transmitted. Zero form data is stored.
